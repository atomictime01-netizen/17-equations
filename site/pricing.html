Pricing questions

Why free, and for how long.

Why is it free?

It started as a way to make seventeen ideas tangible, and charging would mean accounts, servers and tracking, none of which the project needs. It runs entirely in your browser.

Will it stay free?

The code already published stays MIT licensed whatever happens next, so anyone can keep a copy running. I will not put the existing labs behind a paywall.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. The MIT licence allows commercial use, modification and redistribution. Keep the copyright and licence notice. Dependencies and bundled fonts carry their own permissive licences, listed in the README. This is general information, not legal advice.

Where does a tip go?

Straight to the maintainer through Buy Me a Coffee, which takes its own fee. Tips are not tax-deductible donations.
